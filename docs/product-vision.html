02Product vision — BuildHome

A mobile-first platform that accompanies the Indian homeowner from "I want to build a home" → handover → 10 years of maintenance. Eight core differentiators that together create a moat no Indian competitor currently has.

① Regulatory Engine

RERA & Approval Workflow

Digitize the entire regulatory chain: building plan sanction, CLU, RERA registration, Fire NOC, environmental clearance, utility connections. Auto-generate RERA quarterly progress reports. Milestone-based payment tracking aligned with RERA escrow requirements (70% fund ring-fencing). State-by-state compliance logic (MahaRERA, Karnataka RERA, TNRERA, etc.).

Moat: Once your project approvals are in the system, switching cost is near-infinite. This is the lock-in.

② WhatsApp-Native Architecture

Construction Coordination Hub

500M+ Indians use WhatsApp for construction coordination — the app meets them there. Snap a snag photo → WhatsApp → auto-logged with AI tagging. Progress updates from site photos sent via WhatsApp voice note. Vendor communication, approval requests, payment confirmations — all via WhatsApp. Works without app download (progressive engagement). Notifications in the user's language.

Moat: Usage habit stickiness. Once the WhatsApp thread is the source of truth, no one switches.

③ Multi-Language + Regional Adaptation

Speaks Your Language

8 Indian languages at launch (Hindi, Tamil, Kannada, Telugu, Malayalam, Marathi, Bengali, Gujarati). Region-specific terminology mapping — "slab" vs "roof", "cent" vs "sq yard" vs "anna". Voice input for snags in local languages. UI adapts to regional construction practices (Tamil Nadu snag culture, Kerala post-monsoon inspection norms, North vs South pricing conventions).

④ Financial Spine

RERA-Compliant Payment Engine

Auto-calculate milestone payment schedules per RERA norms (foundation → plinth → slab → brickwork → plaster → flooring → finishing → handover). Bank coordination: generate engineer certification → trigger home loan disbursement. Escrow tracking and verification. GST calculation (1%/5%/12% slabs + input credit). Home loan application & tracking via bank partnerships.

⑤ Procurement Marketplace

Buy Smarter Together

Individual homeowners pay retail — BuildHome aggregates demand across projects for bulk pricing. Verified local dealers with real-time stock & pricing. Price comparison + quality certification (brand vs generic, grade verification). Order tracking from factory to site. Post-delivery inspection workflow. Import concierge for premium materials (EUR ex-works → landed INR).

⑥ AI Site Intelligence

Computer Vision for Construction

Upload a site photo → AI identifies current stage (excavation, foundation, plinth, slab, brickwork, plaster, flooring). Snag auto-detection from photos (cracks, seepage, tile misalignment, paint defects). Price prediction for materials (steel/cement trends based on market data). Contractor quality scoring from past project outcomes.

⑦ Snag Lifecycle

Homeowner-Led Quality Assurance

Open a snag from phone camera → auto-categorize → assign to contractor. Photo + voice note in local language. SLA tracking (critical → 24 hr, high → 72 hr). Sign-off with photo evidence. Rolling snagging by stage (RCC, masonry, plaster, waterproofing, tiling, painting) — not just at handover. History follows the home, not the contractor.

⑧ Home OS

Post-Construction for Life

Centralized warranty tracker for all materials + workmanship. Maintenance calendar (service water tank, service AC, repaint timeline, septic tank cleaning). 10-year structural defect monitoring based on monsoon-cycle inspection reminders. Service provider network for repairs. "Home health score" based on age + maintenance history. A 10+ year engagement with every home built through the platform.

04The competitive moat

Four structural advantages that compound over time, making it increasingly hard for incumbents (Livspace, Brick & Bolt) or new entrants to replicate.

Data Network Effect

More projects → better price predictions, accurate contractor ratings, real material cost benchmarks, reliable timeline estimates. Each new project makes the platform more valuable for the next user.

Regulatory Embed

Once a project's RERA filings, approvals, escrow accounts, and bank linkages are in the system, the cost of switching to any alternative approaches infinity. This is structural lock-in.

WhatsApp Habit Stickiness

The daily coordination thread (snag photos, progress updates, approvals) lives in WhatsApp, integrated with the app. Breaking that habit to switch tools means breaking the communication channel itself.

Post-Construction Lock-In

Your home's warranty, maintenance history, service bookings, and manufacturer contacts live in the app. A 10+ year engagement with every home — switching means losing your home's digital record.

06Phased product roadmap

Each phase funds and derisks the next. Build only what the market proves it needs. Current codebase puts you well ahead of a blank-slate start — estimate 30–40% shorter timelines vs. building from scratch.

Phase 0

Multi-Tenant Foundation

Now

Refactor from single-project hardcoded data to multi-tenant architecture. This is the prerequisite for everything else.

  • Add tenant/project/user tables to Supabase
  • Replace data/*.ts imports with dynamic DB queries in repository layer (API mode already exists)
  • Extend Supabase Auth with role-based access (homeowner, architect, contractor, admin)
  • Add project-switcher UI to portal
  • Deploy API on Render/Railway with persistent Postgres

Est. 4–6 weeks. Leverages existing repository pattern + API architecture.

Phase 1

Mobile Web + WhatsApp Bot

Next

Make the portal work on mobile (responsive) and meet users on WhatsApp — the de facto construction communication tool in India.

  • Mobile-responsive DataTable, forms, dashboard
  • WhatsApp Business API integration — inbound photos auto-log as snags or progress updates
  • WhatsApp notifications: snag assigned, milestone due, payment approved, decision needed
  • Voice note → text transcription for snags in local languages
  • Photo capture from within mobile web app

Est. 4 weeks. Highest impact per engineering hour — this is where the habit forms.

Phase 2

Snag Workflow + Progress Timeline

Next

Core construction tracking — the features that save the most time and disputes on-site.

  • Rich snag workflow: photo + voice + location → auto-categorize → assign → SLA → verify → sign-off
  • Progress timeline with photo feed from site (WhatsApp integration already in place)
  • Stage-based snagging (RCC, masonry, plaster, waterproofing, tiling, painting — separate per stage)
  • Snag dashboard: open vs resolved by priority, stage, contractor

Est. 3 weeks. The data model already exists in data/snags.ts and data/progress.ts — productize existing schema.

Phase 3

RERA Compliance Engine

Later

The structural moat — regulatory embed no competitor can replicate quickly.

  • Milestone payment scheduler aligned with RERA norms (foundation → plinth → slab → brickwork → plaster → flooring → finishing → handover)
  • Auto-generate RERA quarterly progress reports (QPRs) from project data
  • Bank coordination workflow: engineer/architect certification → bank disbursement trigger
  • Escrow account tracking: 70% fund ring-fencing verification
  • State-specific compliance variants (MahaRERA, Karnataka RERA, TNRERA, etc.)
  • GST calculation engine (1%/5%/12% + input credit optimization)

Est. 6 weeks. This is the part that requires regulatory research as much as coding.

Phase 4

Material Marketplace MVP

Later

Aggregate demand for better pricing and verified quality — the transactional revenue engine.

  • Onboard verified local dealers (cement, steel, tiles, plumbing, electrical, paint, hardware)
  • Price comparison across dealers with real-time stock
  • Demand aggregation: group similar orders from multiple projects → bulk pricing
  • Order tracking: factory dispatch → transit → site delivery
  • Post-delivery inspection workflow with photo evidence
  • Import concierge: EUR/USD ex-works → estimated landed INR (you already have landedFromEUR() utility)

Est. 8 weeks. Marketplace dynamics are hard — start with curated dealers, not an open marketplace.

Phase 5

Native Mobile Apps

Later

Native experience for on-site photography, offline operation, and push notifications.

  • React Native (or Expo) apps for iOS + Android
  • Offline-first: snag capture, photo taking, notes — sync when online
  • Camera integration with auto-categorization
  • Push notifications (augmenting WhatsApp, not replacing it)
  • QR code scanning for material delivery verification

Est. 8–12 weeks. Mobile web may be sufficient for early adopters — validate before investing in native.

Phase 6

AI Features

Later

Computer vision and predictive models that compound the data advantage.

  • Stage classification from site photos (which construction stage is this?)
  • Snag auto-detection: cracks, seepage, tile misalignment, paint defects from photos
  • Material price prediction (steel/cement trends via market data + historical project data)
  • Contractor quality scoring from snag resolution patterns across projects
  • Timeline prediction: estimated completion based on progress rate vs similar projects

Est. 8 weeks. Requires dataset that earlier phases generate — don't start before Phase 2 is live with real usage.

08Honest risks

RiskMitigation
Multi-tenant refactor is harder than it looks. Extracting hardcoded singletons into a tenant-aware service while keeping the existing site working requires careful abstraction.The repository pattern was designed for exactly this. Create a new MultiTenantRepository implementation that uses the same interface as the current one. Run both in parallel during migration.
WhatsApp Business API has platform risk. Meta controls the API terms, pricing, and access. Policy changes could break the core workflow.Abstract the notification layer. Start with WhatsApp as the primary channel but design for Telegram/RCS as fallback. Don't hardcode WhatsApp-specific logic into business code.
Regulatory complexity is underestimated. Every state has different RERA forms, approval timelines, fees, and processes. Full compliance is a legal product as much as a technical one.Launch with Karnataka RERA (your home state) first. Add states based on user demand. Partner with a construction law firm for compliance review. Never claim "legal advice" — position as "tracking tool" that helps you prepare filings.
Marketplace chicken-and-egg problem. Dealers won't list without buyers; buyers won't come without dealers.Start with the demand aggregation model (group orders from your own users) before opening a dealer marketplace. Phase 4 follows Phase 1–2 for a reason: build the user base first.
AI features require data you don't have yet. Computer vision models need thousands of labelled site photos. Price prediction needs transaction data.Don't build Phase 6 until Phase 2 generates real usage data. Start with heuristic rules (not ML) for stage classification. Label photos as a byproduct of user interactions.
Homeowner willingness to pay. Indian homeowners are price-sensitive. A ₹2,499/project subscription may face resistance against "free" (WhatsApp + Excel).The free tier is the conversion funnel. The pain of coordination must become acute before payment feels worth it. Price at the point where the alternative (hiring a project manager) costs 10× more.
Incumbents have capital. Livspace ($450M+ raised) or Brick & Bolt could copy features.They're structurally supply-side — their revenue comes from builders/vendors, not homeowners. Building a homeowner-trust brand requires a different DNA. The regulatory moat (Phase 3) is hard to replicate quickly across states.
